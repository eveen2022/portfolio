I've been working remotely for three years now, and my opinions on it have shifted more than once.

## What Works

**Deep work blocks.** No one taps your shoulder mid-flow. If I need four uninterrupted hours to untangle a gnarly bug, I can just take them.

## What Doesn't

*Onboarding.* Ramping up a new hire over video calls is genuinely harder than sitting next to them for a week.

Overall, I'd still choose remote — but I don't think it's a free lunch the way it's sometimes sold.
